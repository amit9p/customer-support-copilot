Question : My Galaxy Buds3 Pro left earbud won't connect. Is this a known issue, and has the same problem been resolved for another customer? What fixed it?
Routing  : intents=['product', 'past_issue'] -> indexes=['product_index', 'ticket_index']
Answer   : Yes. This is a known Galaxy Buds3 Pro issue. Another customer had the same problem after switching phones; resetting the earbuds and pairing them again as a new device fixed it. Question : A customer wants to change the address after the order shipped. What is the official process, and do we have a resolved case like this?
Routing  : intents=['order', 'past_issue'] -> indexes=['order_index', 'ticket_index']
Answer   : After shipping, the address cannot be changed by Dandes Electronics or support. The customer can ask the courier to reschedule delivery to the same address, or refuse delivery and place a new order with the correct address. A resolved case followed this process: the customer refused delivery, placed a new order, and received a refund after the original package reached the warehouse.
Sources  : OG-002, TCK-1004

Question : A customer received a damaged phone and wants a refund instead of a replacement. What is the official process, and do we have a previous resolved case where a refund was issued?
Routing  : intents=['policy', 'order', 'past_issue'] -> indexes=['policy_index', 'order_index', 'ticket_index']
Answer   : Report the damage within 48 hours of delivery: open the order on the Orders page and select **Report a Problem**. Upload photos of the phone, box and shipping label; include an unboxing video if available. Pickup is scheduled within 2 business days, followed by a quality check. For a phone, a refund is available only if a replacement unit is unavailable; if the same model is out of stock, the customer can choose a refund or a different colour of that model.

Yes. In a resolved iPhone 16 case, the phone arrived with a cracked screen and a refund was issued after the quality check confirmed damage and a replacement in the same colour was unavailable.
Sources  : RR-003, OG-005, RR-005, TCK-1002
